== Unity Capture (UnityCaptureFilter32.dll / UnityCaptureFilter64.dll) ==
The virtual camera driver. MIT License
https://github.com/schellingb/UnityCapture

Cam Multiplier talks to this driver through its documented shared-memory interface
(unitycapture_sender.py), no code of the driver is included in the Python files.
